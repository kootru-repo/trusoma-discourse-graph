Spread of the whole-body curl in living fetuses at the model age: the cranial-dorsal-hip angle (crown, the back at the level of the mandible, rump) in 2186 crown-rump length images at 12+4 weeks (12+1 to 12+6), crown-rump length 60.5 mm (56.0-64.9): median 124.5 deg, quartiles 119.1-130.1; 95% reference range of the neutral position 118.3-137.8; hyperflexed 22.3%, neutral 73.9%, hyperextended 3.8%. A neck angle in nuchal translucency scans (anterior neck to lower jaw): 56.18 +- 10.87 deg, normal posture group (Peng 2024, n = 360, selected). Check of the model: the same construction on the resting body gives 133.1 deg and crown-rump 61.7 mm at the curl scale 1.0 (setting.body7.Body7.__init__.c_curve); 136.9 at 0.8 and 132-133 at 1.1-1.3. So the bound rest curl is inside the neutral reference range and above the upper quartile; the scale cannot reach the median in this construction. Images are selected for measurement, not sampled at rest.
