Method lead, not a value source: fetal SMPL (Fetuses Made Simple: Modeling and Tracking of Fetal Shape and Pose, Yingcheng Liu, 2025, MICCAI, arXiv:2506.17858; README and paper HTML read, code and weights not). Shape and pose model fitted to MRI segmentations and 16 keypoints; trained at 24-37 weeks from an infant model (SMIL), 3 mm voxels. No value usable at 12.5 weeks.

Why it matters: Its fitting method could measure pose and segment lengths from first-trimester 3D data if such data is found; its tracked poses at 24 weeks and above could bound joint coupling (M-098) for growth past 12.5 weeks.

To resolve: Only if first-trimester 3D volumes or keypoints are found (Kyoto collection MRI, 4D ultrasound): check whether the code runs on them without the unlinked trained weights. Never take shape or pose values from the trained model for the 12.5 week body.

Status: in-progress; priority: P3; category: L.

Evidence so far: 2026-11-13 repository cloned to local/refs/fetal-smpl-src (commit 472b1c4) and its 3,621 lines and bundled arrays read; paper HTML read. IN HAND: fitting code (alignment.py, learn_shape.py, lbs_unpose.py: template mesh fitted to a target surface and joint keypoints with an elastic term; unposing; mesh measures head and abdominal circumference, femur length, body length, volume); one tracked fetus MAP-C507 (100 frames, 16 keypoints; forearm to upper arm 0.91 joint to joint, late gestation, age not given; about 3 s per frame per Vasung 2023 doi:10.1159/000528757, too slow to time a 1.6 s movement); infant (SMIL) pose prior mean and precision factor (spread of 1-3 deg at the shoulder, not usable as joint coupling). NOT IN HAND: trained fetal model (no file, no release), SMIL template (registration), pytorch3d and smplx not installed. No value for the 12.5 week body. USES: (1) automated refit of the sculpt to the physics body, the open part of M-103; (2) the paper's mesh measures applied to the physics mesh against 12-13 week biometry; (3) measuring pose and segment lengths if first-trimester 3D volumes are found (one web search: none public).
