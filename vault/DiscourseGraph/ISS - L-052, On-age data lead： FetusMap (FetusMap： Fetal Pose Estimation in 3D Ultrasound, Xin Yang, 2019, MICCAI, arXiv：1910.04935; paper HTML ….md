On-age data lead: FetusMap (FetusMap: Fetal Pose Estimation in 3D Ultrasound, Xin Yang, 2019, MICCAI, arXiv:1910.04935; paper HTML read): 152 fetuses at 10-14 weeks, 3D ultrasound at 0.5 mm voxels, 16 landmarks over head, neck, shoulder, elbow, wrist, spine, sacrum, hip, knee, ankle; landmark error 4.92 mm. Extension FetusMapV2 (2023, Medical Image Analysis, arXiv:2310.19293): 1,000 volumes, 22 landmarks (search summary only, not read). Data not stated public.

Why it matters: Joint-to-joint lengths and resting joint angles at 10-14 weeks would address the arm rest posture (M-098) and the forearm link (M-102); the only on-age item in the USER's list of fetal 3D models (2026-11-13).

To resolve: Find how the landmark coordinates can be obtained (release, request by the USER, agreement); read FetusMapV2 and its data statement; nothing is sourced until the coordinates are in hand and measured with an error estimate.

Status: blocked; priority: P1; category: L.

Evidence so far: 2026-11-13 access research. FetusMapV2 read in full (arXiv:2310.19293v1; published Medical Image Analysis 2024, doi:10.1016/j.media.2023.103013): 1,000 singleton volumes at 10-15 weeks, voxels 0.5 or 0.3 mm, 22 landmarks (head and trunk 1-10, each arm 3, each leg 3), mean landmark error 3.46 mm, wrists 5.09 and 4.59 mm; it reports no limb lengths, joint angles or pose statistics, only measurement errors (humerus 7.5-7.9 mm, femur 5.8-7.0 mm, crown-rump 6.28 mm). No data or code statement, no repository, no release or challenge found (4 web searches; the publisher page returned 403, so its data statement is unread). Corresponding authors Dong Ni and Weijun Huang, Shenzhen University; scans from partner hospitals under local IRB; company affiliate Shenzhen RayShape. Only route found: a request by the USER for the landmark coordinates with gestational age and voxel size, or for the authors to run our measuring script. No public substitute at 10-14 weeks found.

Note: FetusMap read in full 2026-11-15 (FETUSMAP_READING_2026-11-15.md): dataset of 152 volumes, 10-14 weeks, 16 manual landmarks, but the paper gives no segment lengths, joint angles or per-week statistics, and no data or code release statement. No value can be sourced from the paper itself. Only the authors' manual annotations could be (a request is the USER's to send); network predictions have 4.92 mm mean error (up to 9.40 mm), too large for limb segments of 1-2 cm.
