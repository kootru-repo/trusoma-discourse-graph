Hand-to-mouth and hand-to-eye movements of the human fetus at 14 weeks: a movement begins with the hand stationary on the chest (below the shoulders, above the belly) and ends when it stops on the mouth or eye; to the mouth it takes 1640 ms, to the eye 1893 ms. Analysed movements in 8 fetuses, 20 minutes each: 28 to the mouth and 20 to the eye, so at least 10.5 to the mouth and 7.5 to the eye per hour (selected movements only: a lower bound). The wrist was tracked; no joint angles. For the model: the validation target for the hand finding the mouth belongs at 14 weeks, not 12.5; at 12.5 weeks the read source counts hand-face contact as one class (de Vries 1985).
