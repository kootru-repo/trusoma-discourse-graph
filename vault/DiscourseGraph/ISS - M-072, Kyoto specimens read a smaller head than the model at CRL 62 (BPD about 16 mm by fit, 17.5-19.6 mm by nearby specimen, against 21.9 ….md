Kyoto specimens read a smaller head than the model at CRL 62 (BPD about 16 mm by fit, 17.5-19.6 mm by nearby specimen, against 21.9 mm), so body widths taken as ratios and as millimetres disagree.

Why it matters: Shoulder width 22.5 or 16.6 mm and hip separation 9.2 or 6.7 mm depending on which is used; affects M-068, M-069 and HIP_FRAC. Cause of the head difference not read.

To resolve: Before the growth chart rewrite, find whether specimen fixation or the BPD fit explains the difference, then choose ratio or absolute for every Kyoto-derived width.

Status: verified; priority: P2; category: M.

Evidence so far: 2026-11-05: choice made, cause not proven. Kyoto BPD/CRL 0.251 (n=13, CRL 38-225) against 0.355 in the model. Fit ruled out: local ratio gives 15.2-16.4 mm. Formalin wrong direction or too small: Schultz 1919 head breadth +4.8%, sitting height -2.5%; Takakuwa 2021 2.7-3.5% shrinkage. Chambers 1993 abstract: autopsy BPD matches ultrasound. Katsube 2025: storage compression in some specimens, unmeasured. Suzuki 2019 ossified shaft against chart: -31% at CRL 62, -1% at CRL 185, while BPD stays -26% to -13%, so at large sizes the deficit is in the head; the test cannot separate causes at CRL 62. Decision: Kyoto skeletal widths in millimetres at CRL: shoulder 16.6 mm, hips 6.7 mm, with 22.5 and 9.2 mm as upper bounds. Applied at the growth chart rewrite. See LIKELY_SOURCEABLE.md.
