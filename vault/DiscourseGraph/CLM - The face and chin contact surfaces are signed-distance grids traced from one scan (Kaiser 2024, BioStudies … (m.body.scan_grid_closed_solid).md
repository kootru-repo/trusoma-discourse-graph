The face and chin contact surfaces are signed-distance grids traced from one scan (Kaiser 2024, BioStudies S-BSST1227, 13 week head). Each grid covers a box and the face grid is cut below the mouth line and above the brow, where other parts answer. The solid a grid describes ends at those cuts and at the box faces with a flat closing surface, and the stored field is the signed distance to that closed solid; outside the box the lookup continues the field from the nearest box point (value there plus the distance to it). No value is added: the same scan data, the same cuts. WHY: The build (face_sdf_build.py step 5) had overwritten the interior in the cut zones with '5 mm outside', and the lookup answered 'far' outside the box, so at each cut and at each box face the solid reaches the field stepped from clear to as much as 9 mm (face) or 6.4 mm (chin) inside within one 0.3 mm cell: 5,595 such steps in the face grid, 2,882 and 6,416 inside nodes on the box faces. A hand sphere crossing a step was at once deep inside with no surface direction (zero gradient), which gave violent corrections and, when the row was forced, a non-finite contact normal and the stop 'non-finite state' (audit item M-101). LIMIT: the closing surfaces are flat and lie half a cell beyond the last inside node; where the real skin continues outside a grid box (side of the cheek, under the jaw) the model has a flat wall, not skin. Inside ridges of the field keep some zero-gradient nodes (77 face, 202 chin within 1 mm of the surface); a row there is skipped, not solved.
