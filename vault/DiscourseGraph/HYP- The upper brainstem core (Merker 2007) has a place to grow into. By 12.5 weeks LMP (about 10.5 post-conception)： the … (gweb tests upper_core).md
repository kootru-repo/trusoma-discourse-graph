The upper brainstem core (Merker 2007) has a place to grow into. By 12.5 weeks LMP (about 10.5 post-conception): the superior colliculus is beginning to laminate with retinal fibers in its superficial layers; midbrain dopamine neurons have projections (7.5 PCW) and have reached the putamen (9 PCW), though the SN, VTA and red nucleus become distinct nuclei only from 15 PCW; striatal D1 and D2 projection neurons and patch compartments are present (7-11 PCW); the dorsal raphe holds serotonin neurons; hypothalamic POMC and TRH appear at GW10 and KISS1 at GW12, with oxytocin and vasopressin later (GW16 transcripts; Yew reports immunoreactivity from 12-14 weeks); the prethalamus is forming the zona incerta, marked as such at 15 PCW. The web must reach their presence as regions by regional identity (S.fate), never by schedule. Their function (target map, selection, motivation) is not a Phase 1 target; it belongs to Merker plan M5 and M6. Not sourced for this age: PAG, midbrain reticular formation.
