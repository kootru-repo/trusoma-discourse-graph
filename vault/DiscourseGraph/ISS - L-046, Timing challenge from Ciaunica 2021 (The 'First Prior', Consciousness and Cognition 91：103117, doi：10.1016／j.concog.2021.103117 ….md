Timing challenge from Ciaunica 2021 (The 'First Prior', Consciousness and Cognition 91:103117, doi:10.1016/j.concog.2021.103117, author manuscript read 2026-11-07, p13-14, marked 'Speculatively'): before thalamocortical connections (~20 wk) the fetus acts in a reflex, automatic mode; correct somatosensory-motor coupling 'only possible after the 20th week'; the fetus is homeostatic, allostasis develops after week 20 (the mother is the allostatic partner). Lagercrantz 2009 (Pediatr Res 65:255, doi:10.1203/PDR.0b013e3181973b0d, abstract only): fetal reactions are probably preprogrammed and subcortical.

Why it matters: Bears on the Phase 1 premise (learner learns the body map at 12.5 wk) and puts allostasis after 20 wk, i.e. outside the 12.5 scope; consistent with the learner's brainstem home hypothesis, since neither paper excludes brainstem-level learning.

To resolve: Check the cited primaries (Hevner 2000, Eyre 2000, Okado 1980) for what learning, if any, is possible at 12.5 wk below the cortex; record the answer in the growth plan; keep allostasis deferred with trigger 'post-20-week phase'.

Status: open; priority: P2; category: L.
