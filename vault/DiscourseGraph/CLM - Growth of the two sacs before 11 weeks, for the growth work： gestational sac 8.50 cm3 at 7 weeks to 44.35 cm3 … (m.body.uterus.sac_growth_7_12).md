Growth of the two sacs before 11 weeks, for the growth work: gestational sac 8.50 cm3 at 7 weeks to 44.35 cm3 at 10 weeks (Rolo 2009, n = 74); gestational and amniotic sac volumes at 6-12 weeks correlate with age and crown-rump length, amniotic sac mean 3.69 mL (0.01-92.1), gestational sac mean 20.35 mL (0.7-113) (Odeh 2008, n = 142). With amniotic fluid 23.33 cm3 at 10 weeks (Rolo 2010) the amniotic sac is about half the gestational sac at 10 weeks and close to all of it by 12.5.
