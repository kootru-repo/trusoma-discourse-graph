Deferred comparators from the MEM manuscript (Starzyk & Galus 2026, revised manuscript, no journal or DOI): (1) prototype map with re-entry, eq 6 match + competition + top-down reinstatement into the receptor map, a map-forming comparator once its reconstruction step is checked against the no-generative rule; (2) allostatic violation vector, tanh affect and quadratic cost (eqs 2-4), with homeostatic RL (Keramati & Gutkin 2014), for the deferred state network; (3) program search with an improvement threshold (eq 31, theta_imp) for later movement phases. Also its protocol: matched budgets, ablation with capacity-matched controls, multiple seeds.

Why it matters: Comparisons are free (USER 2026-11-02), but none of this applies at the 12.5 week map-and-movement scope; the full equations are in Galus & Starzyk 2026 (arXiv), not read.

To resolve: When proto-allostasis or post-12.5 movement is un-deferred, or a second map comparator is wanted: read the arXiv model, implement as a comparison model outside the brain package, never as the TruSoma learner.
