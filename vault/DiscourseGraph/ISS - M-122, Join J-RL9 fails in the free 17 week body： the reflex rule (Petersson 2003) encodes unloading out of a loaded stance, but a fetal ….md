Join J-RL9 fails in the free 17 week body: the reflex rule (Petersson 2003) encodes unloading out of a loaded stance, but a fetal twitch in fluid mostly loads skin (unloading share 0.07). With no noise the rule reaches only 0.57-0.68 against the moving body's own map over 10,000 epochs (RESULT_TR13_SNR_OFFLINE_2026-11-15.md); TR13c-f and TR13e all fail.

Why it matters: Part B cannot meet TR13 by any twitch strength or rest; the method itself needs a fetal-stage source.

To resolve: Source what a fetal or neonatal twitch teaches when its signal is contact made (twitch reafference: Blumberg lab, fetal rat), or evidence that the fetus rests loaded against the wall during twitches; then re-specify PB2 and re-register TR13. TR12 (yoke, Robinson 2005) is independent and can be built next.
