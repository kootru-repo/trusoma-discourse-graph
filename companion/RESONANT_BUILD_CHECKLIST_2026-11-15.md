## Final verdict on the items marked NOT (2026-11-15)
Each NOT is blocked by a missing source or a missing prior piece, not by effort: 17-18 need a sourced consolidation trigger, arbor-extension rule, reach and nucleus size (M-121, M-116; Waite 2000, Rajan 1999 and the FetusMap and de Bakker readings contain none); 19 T-type values (none found); 20 a fetal-stage reflex rule (M-122, M-118; the source species tune it after birth); 21 the rat-to-human date convention (open in DESIGN section 9); 22 a coupling mechanism for relay burst synchrony (Ho 1999 full text closed); 25 TR7 needs the assembled learner's drive and read-out, both unsourced; 26 and 16b need a learner that drives the limbs; 27 needs all of the above. Nothing else in the plan, METHODS sections 3, 4, 7, 8 and DESIGN sections 7 and 10, is buildable from the sources in hand.

## Source sweep for the remaining gaps (2026-11-15, RESONANT_GAPS_SOURCING_2026-11-15.md; queries and results listed there)
- Item 19 T-type Ca2+: PARTIAL, not buildable. Neonatal rat (P12-17) laterodorsal thalamus gives m2h kinetics, tau_m 6.3-7.3 ms, tau_h 32-69 ms and half-inactivation -85.5 and -98.1 mV (Tarasenko 1997, abstract only); Coulter 1989 and Huguenard 1992 have fitted sets but the animals' ages are unverified (adult values are excluded); no activation half-voltage, slope or conductance density in hand; the trigeminal principal nucleus has presence-only evidence (Lo 1999, Lo 2001, no voltage clamp). A current built from these would need three unsourced values.
- Item 22 S3 relay burst synchrony: PARTIAL, not buildable. PrV bursts exist E16 to P8, are synchronous and are abolished by bicuculline or kynurenic acid (Ho and Waite 1999, Waite 2000, abstracts); rate, duration, interval, participants and the synchronising mechanism (gap junctions) are NOT FOUND for PrV. Only a spinal analogue exists (Momose-Sato 2013: interval 2 min at E14.5 to 3-13 min at E17.5), a different circuit.
- Items 16b and 26 yoke learning arm and TR12: PARTIAL, not buildable. The verbatim definition of a conjugate movement and of passive dragging, the score 2 x CLM / hindlimb movements and the 0.1 s timestamp precision are found (Robinson 2008, 2015; Kleven 2009, 2014; already used in yoke_test.py); numeric movement criteria (displacement, speed, duration, quiet interval, how parallel) and any learning rule with equations are NOT FOUND (the account is verbal: an error signal and selection); Robinson 2005 and Kleven 2004 full texts are closed.
- Items 17, 18, 20, 25, 27: unchanged (M-121, M-116, M-122, M-118; TR7 has no independent read-out without the missing learner components).
Result: after the sweep no item marked NOT has a source that makes it buildable; each remaining gap names the exact missing quantity and where it would be (a closed full text or an unmeasured quantity).

## Correction to the sweep (2026-11-15, later)
The sourcing agent reported Tarasenko 1997 and Coulter 1989 as closed. Semantic Scholar lists both as open (PMC1159338 and PMC1189159), the PMC PDF links need a browser challenge that curl alone fails, and both were then read in full from the scans. Coulter 1989 (2- to 25-day-old Wistar rats) gives the full T-type gating, so item 19 is built. Kleven 2004 also has an author copy (works.bepress.com/gale_kleven/21); Robinson 2005 and Ho and Waite 1999 have no open copy found (Semantic Scholar, Europe PMC).
